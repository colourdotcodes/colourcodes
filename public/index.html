How to mine

Your computer guesses hashes. When one is good enough, the contract checks it and pays a block. You never deposit anything; holding the token is your ticket.

  1. 1
    Hold $CCODE

    Buy at least the minimum on Robinhood Chain and keep it in your wallet.

  2. 2
    Open the Miner

    Enter the computer, open colour.codes Miner and connect your wallet. It switches to Robinhood Chain (id 4663) for you.

  3. 3
    Press Generate Coins

    Your browser starts hashing. Leave the tab open; every good share counts toward the pool's next block.

  4. 4
    Claim once a day

    Open colour.codes Pool and press Claim. Rewards go straight to your wallet.
